**Formal statement.**

Setting: smooth solution of 3D NS on $\mathbb{T}^3$ or $\mathbb{R}^3$, $\Omega(t) := \|\omega(t)\|_{L^\infty}$, attained at $x^*(t)$. Define the peak curvature scale $\ell(t)$ by $\ell^{-2} := \max\big(0, -\Delta|\omega|(x^*,t)\big)/\Omega(t)$, the octave Reynolds number $R(t) := \Omega(t)\ell(t)^2/\nu$, the Biot-Savart split $S = S^{\mathrm{loc}} + S^{\mathrm{far}}$ at radius $K\ell(t)$ around $x^*$ ($K$ a fixed universal constant, say $8$), and the gear ratio $g(t) := |S^{\mathrm{far}}(x^*)| / \max(|S^{\mathrm{loc}}(x^*)|,\ \nu/\ell^2)$. Define the peak production-to-toll ratio
$$q(t) := \frac{\big(\hat\omega^T S \hat\omega\big)(x^*,t)\ \ell(t)^2}{\nu}, \qquad \hat\omega = \omega/|\omega|.$$

*Kinematic spine (exact, from the Kato-inequality maximum principle).* At the peak, $\tfrac{d^+}{dt}\Omega \le \big(\hat\omega^T S\hat\omega\big)(x^*)\,\Omega + \nu\Delta|\omega|(x^*) = (q-1)\,\frac{\nu\,\Omega}{\ell^2}$. So **no doubling of $\Omega$ can begin while $q \le 1$**: the viscous toll at the peak exceeds the realized stretching there.

*Lemma A0 (tollgate; theorem-shaped, VERIFIER target).* There exist $C(K)$ (of size $O(\log K)$, from the cancellation of the Biot-Savart kernel against the curvature-resolved core) and $R_* = R_*(K) > 0$ such that, whenever the gradient microscale at the peak agrees with the curvature scale up to a factor $K$,
$$q \;\le\; C(K)\,(1 + g)\, R, \qquad \text{hence } q \le 1 \text{ whenever } R \le R_* \text{ and } g \le 1.$$
In words: a *self-strained* core at *small octave Reynolds number* cannot amplify its own peak vorticity at all. The toll is paid in the currency $\nu/\ell^2$ and the local stretching supply is capped by $C(K)\,\Omega$.

*Marginal-Reynolds fact.* A viscous self-similar collapse has $\ell \sim \sqrt{\nu(T-t)}$ and $\Omega \sim (T-t)^{-1}$, so $R \equiv O(1)$: the standard collapse route passes the tollgate at the marginal Reynolds number, neither deep in the viscous regime nor far from it.

*Conjecture A1 (the forge claim).* The geometric depletion of stretching (LEARNINGS #10, #14: realized $\int\omega\cdot S\omega \approx 0.53 \int \lambda_3|\omega|^2$, unforced) is the physical content of the gap between the soft constant $R_*(K) \ll 1$ and the needed threshold $R \gtrsim 1$. Precisely: there exists a universal $\delta > 0$ such that for any smooth solution, at any peak with $g \le 1$,
$$\big(\hat\omega^T S\hat\omega\big)(x^*) \;\le\; (1-\delta)\,\frac{\nu}{\ell^2} \quad\text{whenever } R \le 1+\delta,$$
i.e. the effective tollgate extends past the marginal Reynolds number once depletion is priced in, and consequently no blow-up can proceed through self-strained octaves at the self-similar rate. The remaining routes (externally geared octaves, $g > 1$ sustained over unboundedly many octaves, and $R \gg 1$ type-II routes) are the named loopholes; closing them is the open content.

## Card B

**Name.** Hypocoercive twist of the enstrophy by the strain-vorticity cross term: $H_\beta = \tfrac12\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2 - \beta\int\omega\cdot S\omega$.

**Status (self-assessed).** Dead as a closure route. The kill is overdetermined: three independent fatal wounds, each recorded precisely below. The corpse is valuable: it fences off the entire class of constant-coefficient polynomial twisted functionals, and it leaves one named, measurable residue (the shape functional $\Gamma$ below, with explicit measured values).

**Formal statement.** Let $Y := \int \omega\cdot S\omega\,dx$ (the global stretching production) and for $\beta > 0$ define the twisted functional $H_\beta := \tfrac12\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2 - \beta Y$. Using the verified identities (header) and the strain evolution $D_t S = -(S^2 + A^2) - \nabla^2 p + \nu\Delta S$, the exact ledger is
$$\dot Y = \|S\omega\|_{L^2}^2 \;-\; \int \omega^T(\nabla^2 p)\,\omega\,dx \;+\; \nu V, \qquad V := 2\!\int\!\Delta\omega\cdot S\omega + \int\omega\cdot(\Delta S)\,\omega,$$
where the transport contributions cancel exactly by incompressibility, the $A^2$ contribution vanishes pointwise ($\omega^T A^2\omega = 0$, identity 2-3), and $\omega^T S^2 \omega = |S\omega|^2$ (identity 5). Hence
$$\dot H_\beta = Y - \nu\|\nabla\omega\|_{L^2}^2 - \beta\|S\omega\|_{L^2}^2 + \beta\int\omega^T(\nabla^2 p)\,\omega - \beta\nu V.$$
The hypocoercive signature is real: the twist generates the negative quartic $-\beta\|S\omega\|^2$, which dominates the dangerous cubic $Y$ at high amplitude via Young, $Y \le \tfrac{\beta}{2}\|S\omega\|^2 + \tfrac{1}{2\beta}\|\omega\|^2$, and the leftover $\tfrac{1}{2\beta}\|\omega\|^2$ is integrable in time against the energy budget ($\int_0^\infty\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2\,dt \le E_0/\nu$). The would-be theorem ("$H_\beta$ bounded for all time, hence enstrophy controlled, hence regularity by subcriticality of $\dot H^1$") then dies on the following trichotomy, each branch checked:

1. **Coercivity wound (fixed $\beta$).** $H_\beta$ is not equivalent to the enstrophy: $|\beta Y|$ exceeds $\tfrac12\|\omega\|^2$ exactly at high amplitude. Raw scaling degrees: $\|\omega\|_2^2 \sim \lambda^{1}$, $Y \sim \lambda^{3}$. The mismatch is exactly $\lambda^2$, one amplitude order of $\nabla u$. Bounding $\dot H_\beta$ bounds nothing about $\|\omega\|_2$ precisely in the regime where the bound is needed.
2. **Normalization wound (adaptive $\beta$).** Restoring norm equivalence requires $\beta(t) \le c/\|S(t)\|_\infty$ (the unique time-dimensioned normalizer available). The Young step then re-imports the coefficient $\tfrac{1}{2\beta} = \tfrac{\|S\|_\infty}{2c}$ in front of $\|\omega\|^2$, and Gronwall returns "regularity if $\int\|S\|_\infty\,dt < \infty$": Beale-Kato-Majda (1984), exactly. The twist buys back the bare estimate. Circular.
3. **Pressure and viscous-commutator wounds (no sign).** The leftover quartic $\int\omega^T(\nabla^2 p)\,\omega$ has the same raw degree $\lambda^{5}$ as the gain $\|S\omega\|^2$ ($\nabla^2 p \sim \lambda^4$ times $|\omega|^2 \sim \lambda^4$, integrated $\lambda^{-3}$), so only a sharp-constant comparison could save branch 1, and the measured sign is indefinite (probe below). Independently, the viscous cross term obeys only $|V| \lesssim \|S\|_\infty\|\nabla\omega\|^2 + \dots$, re-importing the BKM coefficient through the dissipative term even where the pressure term cooperates.

**The kill, stated as the cross-field diagnosis.** Hypocoercivity is a fixed-amplitude-order technology: in kinetic Fokker-Planck, transport is linear, the whole problem lives among quadratic forms, and the commutator $[A,B]$ transfers dissipation between *directions* at fixed order. The NS degeneracy is not directional ($\nu\Delta$ acts on all of space) but across *amplitude orders*: stretching enters one order above dissipation, the "operator" $S = S[\omega]$ carries a power of the solution, and every commutator gain inherits that extra power. A twist strong enough to absorb the stretching term is exactly too strong to be relatively bounded, with mismatch exponent $\lambda^2$, independent of the base norm chosen (the mismatch is the degree of the nonlinearity, not an artifact of $L^2$). Consistent check from the literature: hypocoercivity does serve NS where the problem is linearized (Gallay-Wayne on the Oseen vortex, Beck-Wayne metastability, the enhanced-dissipation literature), i.e. precisely where the amplitude order is fixed.

**Cross-field source.** Villani, *Hypocoercivity* (AMS Memoirs, 2009); Dolbeault-Mouhot-Schmeiser (2015) twisted-$L^2$ method. Strain evolution and pressure-Hessian closure problem: Vieillefosse (1982), Cantwell (1992), Meneveau (Annu. Rev. Fluid Mech. 2011).
