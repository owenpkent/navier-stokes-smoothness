## 1. Identity audit (a): GR Card B, the backward-kernel enstrophy identity

Claim: for $G = G^u_{x_0,t_0}$ solving $\partial_t G + u\cdot\nabla G + \nu\Delta G = 0$ (Constantin-Iyer backward density), $\frac{d}{dt}\int|\omega|^2 G\,dx = -2\nu\int|\nabla\omega|^2 G + 2\int(\omega\cdot S\omega)\,G$, zero error terms.

Derivation (by hand):

1. Kernel: $\partial_t G = -u\cdot\nabla G - \nu\Delta G$.
2. Vorticity equation dotted with $2\omega$: $\partial_t|\omega|^2 + u\cdot\nabla|\omega|^2 = 2\nu\,\omega\cdot\Delta\omega + 2\,\omega\cdot S\omega$ (using $\omega\cdot(\omega\cdot\nabla u) = \omega\cdot S\omega$).
3. $\frac{d}{dt}\int|\omega|^2 G = \int(\partial_t|\omega|^2)G + \int|\omega|^2\partial_t G$.
4. Substitute 1 and 2: $= \int\big[-u\cdot\nabla|\omega|^2 + 2\nu\,\omega\cdot\Delta\omega + 2\,\omega\cdot S\omega\big]G + \int|\omega|^2\big[-u\cdot\nabla G - \nu\Delta G\big]$.
5. Transport pair: $-\int(u\cdot\nabla|\omega|^2)G - \int|\omega|^2(u\cdot\nabla G) = -\int u\cdot\nabla(|\omega|^2 G) = \int(\nabla\cdot u)|\omega|^2 G = 0$ (incompressibility; torus or decay kills boundary terms).
6. Pointwise identity: $2\,\omega\cdot\Delta\omega = \Delta|\omega|^2 - 2|\nabla\omega|^2$.
7. Viscous cross term: $2\nu\int(\omega\cdot\Delta\omega)G = \nu\int(\Delta|\omega|^2)G - 2\nu\int|\nabla\omega|^2 G = \nu\int|\omega|^2\Delta G - 2\nu\int|\nabla\omega|^2 G$ (integrate by parts twice).
8. The $\nu\int|\omega|^2\Delta G$ from step 7 cancels the $-\nu\int|\omega|^2\Delta G$ from the kernel term in step 4. Exactly.
9. Remainder: $\frac{d}{dt}\int|\omega|^2 G = -2\nu\int|\nabla\omega|^2 G + 2\int(\omega\cdot S\omega)\,G$.

**Verdict on the identity: EXACT, zero error terms.** Load-bearing sign: the cancellation in step 8 requires the BACKWARD equation ($+\nu\Delta G$ on the left). If one used the forward Fokker-Planck sign, the audit leaves an uncancelled error term $+2\nu\int|\omega|^2\Delta G$ and the identity is broken. The Constantin-Iyer backward orientation is not decoration; it is the whole mechanism. Double stochasticity ($\int G\,dx = 1$) holds because $\nabla\cdot u = 0$ makes Lebesgue invariant for the forward adjoint: consistent.

Criticality of $\mathcal{W} = (t_0-t)^2\int|\omega|^2 G$: under $u_\lambda$, $\omega_\lambda = \lambda^2\omega(\lambda x,\lambda^2 t)$, $G^{u_\lambda} = \lambda^3 G^u(\lambda x,\lambda^2 t)$ (it solves the rescaled backward equation and stays a density), so $\int|\omega_\lambda|^2 G^{u_\lambda}dx = \lambda^4\int|\omega|^2 G\,dy$ and $(t_0'-t')^2 = \lambda^{-4}(t_0-t)^2$. $\mathcal{W}$ is invariant: **exactly critical, $a = 0$, no correction needed** since the identity needed none.

**Adverse finding discovered during the audit (fires on conjecture (H), not on B1).** (H) is quantified "for all smooth solutions, all centers, all $t < t_0$". Take a global smooth solution on $\mathbb{T}^3$ and send $t_0 - t \to \infty$ at fixed $t$: $G^u$ mixes to the uniform density (rate at least $\nu\,\times$ Poincare, from $\frac{d}{d\tau}\|G-\bar G\|_2^2 = -2\nu\|\nabla G\|_2^2$), the $(t_0-t)^{-1}$ term vanishes, and (H) degenerates to $\int\omega\cdot S\omega\,dx \le \nu\int|\nabla\omega|^2 dx$, i.e. $dZ/dt \le 0$ for every global smooth solution at every time. That is false: finding #10 measures production exceeding dissipation over an extended interval on the smooth Taylor-Green run, and high-Re axisymmetric no-swirl flows (provably global, Ladyzhenskaya 1968 class) exhibit transient enstrophy growth. The same holds on $\mathbb{R}^3$ with the spreading Gaussian. Worse, the failure is scale-invariantly dense: rescaling a large-$(t_0-t)$ counterexample produces violations at arbitrarily small $t_0 - t$ with small data, so no unconditional smallness-of-window restriction can repair (H). **Conjecture (H) as stated is FALSE.** Repair direction: condition (H) on a scale-invariant intensity threshold at the kernel scale (a local Reynolds number of the $G$-ball), or weaken to quasi-monotonicity of $\mathcal{W}$ with a correction that is negligible only in the intense regime. The unconditional Perelman shape is not available; the probe's job becomes mapping the conditional boundary.

Second flag (coercivity): the cap $\int|\omega|^2 G \lesssim (t_0-t)^{-2}$ converts to local control only with a Gaussian LOWER bound on $G$ over the parabolic ball, which is exactly the off-diagonal/Harnack information that SSSZ 2012 say requires critical drift control. B3 (Nash upper bound) does not supply it. As drafted, the monotone quantity is critical-but-conditionally-coercive, with a circularity risk in the downstream chain.

## 2. Identity audit (b): QI Card 2, the halo lemma

Re-derivation. Stokes on $D(y,s,e)$: $|\oint_{\partial D} u\cdot dl| = |\int_D \omega\cdot e\,dA| \ge \Gamma$. Cauchy-Schwarz: $\Gamma^2 \le 2\pi s\oint_{\partial D}|u|^2 dl$, so $\oint_{\partial D(y,s)}|u|^2 dl \ge \Gamma^2/(2\pi s)$. Integrate $s \in [a, \ell/4]$: each transverse annulus carries line-density energy $\ge (\Gamma^2/2\pi)\ln(\ell/4a)$. Stacking: with $e$ FIXED, the height $z(y) = \gamma(y)\cdot e$ is strictly increasing ($\gamma'\cdot e \ge \cos\frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\sqrt3}{2}$), so each spatial point lies in at most one transverse plane: multiplicity one, no overlap counting needed, and $dV = dA\,dz \ge \frac{\sqrt3}{2}dA\,dy$. Integrating $y$ over the middle half (length $\ell/2$) and checking containment ($\ell + \ell/4 < R$ since $\ell \le R/4$):

$$\int_{B(x_0,R)}|u|^2\,dx \;\ge\; \frac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot\frac{\Gamma^2\ell}{2\pi}\ln\frac{\ell}{4a}.$$

**The Lemma is TRUE as stated, with explicit $c_0 = \sqrt3/4 \approx 0.43$.** It is a clean VERIFIER target. One constant quibble: the small-loop exclusion argument should use swing $\sqrt2$, not $2$ (a closed curve guarantees two tangents with $T\cdot T' \le 0$ since $\oint T\,ds = 0$; antipodal tangents are not guaranteed off symmetric loops). This only tightens $\delta_0(\theta)$: require $\theta(2r) < \sqrt2$.

Unconditional failure factor: confirmed in the card's claimed currency. At fixed $\Gamma$, rescaling sends $\ell \mapsto \lambda^{-1}\ell$, so the octave budget $N \le 4\pi E_0/(c_0\ln2\,\Gamma^2\ell)$ grows like $\lambda$: deficit $\lambda^{-1}$ in the energy, which is the square of the $\lambda^{-1/2}$ norm deficit of LEARNINGS #1. The corpse-ledger entry (dead form 3) is correctly priced.

Weakest link of the conditional chain (the card flags sheet evasion; the audit finds the leak is wider and fires even for tubes). Two independent holes in the step "H1 + H3 feed the Lemma":

1. **Longitudinal ($\ell$-pinning broken as drafted).** H1 forbids small closed intense loops, and lines cannot end, but the line through the concentration point can simply EXIT the intense set $\Omega_t$: the extension argument extends the line, not the intense segment, and H1 (a modulus on $\Omega_t$ only) cannot prevent $\Omega_t$ from being a small ball threaded by long weak lines. Flux through fixed-radius discs decays as the tube bundle spreads beyond $\Omega_t$. Neither H1 nor H3 supplies flux $\ge \Gamma$ along the full length $\delta_0$; the claim "the intense tube extends coherently to length $\ge \delta_0(\theta)$" does not follow from the stated hypotheses. This is the card's "tertiary" death promoted to primary.
2. **Radial (quantifier gap at large $s$).** H3 gives flux on discs of radius $\le \sqrt{\nu(T-t)}$ meeting $\Omega_t$; the Lemma needs flux $\ge \Gamma$ on ALL $s \in [a, \ell/4]$, i.e. out to the pinned macroscopic radius $\delta_0/4$. Sub-intense vorticity (below $\frac12\|\omega\|_\infty$, hence invisible to H1) of opposite sign in an annular sheath cancels outer-radius flux at zero cost: sheath magnitude $\sim\frac12\|\omega\|_\infty$ over area $s^2$ can cancel far more than $\Gamma$. Roll-up morphology (Hou-Luo) realizes exactly this. So the sheet evasion is stronger than recorded: roll-up defeats the chain even when a genuine tube is present at the core.

Consequence: as drafted, the dynamic conjecture does not follow from H1-H3 plus the Lemma; the hypotheses must be strengthened (flux persistence in radius and along the tube), at which point the card is openly a classification result ("any blow-up is circulation-dilute at the active scale, or incoherent at $\beta = 0$, or Type-II"), not a closure. Still a coordinate; not what `alive` advertised.
