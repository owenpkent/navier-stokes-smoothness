## Card 1 (Lens A)

**Name**: No fault-tolerant cascade (the error-correction obstruction to blow-up).

**Status (self-assessed)**: wounded. The naive form (threshold-from-timing) is dead and its corpse is recorded inside this card; the surviving form (compounding infidelity with no recovery channel) is alive but its load-bearing mechanism is unproven and partially overlaps the renormalization/spectral lens.

**Formal statement.**

Setup. On $\mathbb{T}^3$, let $\Delta_j$ be Littlewood-Paley projections, $S_j = \sum_{i \le j} \Delta_i$. Define, for a Leray-Hopf solution $u$:

- shell energy $E_j(t) = \tfrac12 \|\Delta_j u\|_{L^2}^2$, shell velocity $v_j(t) = \|\Delta_j u\|_{L^\infty}$;
- local Reynolds number $\mathrm{Re}_j(t) = \nu^{-1} 2^{-j} v_j(t)$ (the per-eddy inverse "error rate"; $\sup_j 2^{-j} v_j = \|u\|_{\dot B^{-1}_{\infty,\infty}}$, the largest critical norm);
- the flux $\Pi_j(t)$ through frequency $2^j$, defined as the trilinear term in the exact $S_j$-filtered energy identity $\frac{d}{dt} \sum_{i\le j} E_i = -\Pi_j - \nu \|\nabla S_j u\|_{L^2}^2$ (Constantin-E-Titi form);
- the cascade fidelity $F_j(t) = \dfrac{\Pi_j(t)}{2^{j+1} v_j(t) E_j(t)}$: realized flux normalized by the dimensional maximum (shell energy per local turnover time $\tau_j = (2^j v_j)^{-1}$), a dimensionless, scale-invariant efficiency.

Part (i), threshold necessity (classical, recorded for completeness, NOT the conjecture): there is a universal $R_* > 0$ such that if $u$ blows up at $T < \infty$, then along the active front $\limsup_{t \to T} \sup_j \mathrm{Re}_j(t) = \infty$, and passing each octave in the remaining time forces $\bar F_{j_n} \ge F_* > 0$ on a positive fraction of turnover times at the front shells $j_n$ (the timing tax of LEARNINGS #9: flux must at least carry the shell energy down one octave within the time remaining). This half is equivalent to known critical-norm divergence (Leray-type lower bounds) and is not new.

Part (ii), the conjecture (no error-corrected cascade): let $u$ blow up at $T$ with front sequence $(j_n, t_n)$ and rescaled front states $w_n(y, s) = 2^{-j_n} u(2^{-j_n} y,\; t_n + 2^{-2j_n} s)$, viewed in a critical profile space $X$ ($\dot H^{1/2}$ or $\dot B^{-1}_{\infty,\infty}$ locally). Then there exist universal $\kappa > 1$ and $\rho > 0$ such that the octave return map $\mathcal{T}: w_n \mapsto w_{n+1}$ induced by the exact NS flow satisfies: on the $\rho$-neighborhood of any trajectory obeying part (i), the linearization of $\mathcal{T}$ has spectral radius $\ge \kappa$ on the complement of the symmetry directions (scaling, translation, time-shift), and $\mathcal{T}$ admits NO cycle (no scaling-periodic orbit, hence no discretely self-similar blow-up) with $\liminf_n \bar F_{j_n} \ge F_*$ and $\mathrm{Re}_{j_n} \ge R_*$. Consequence: sustaining the above-threshold cascade through infinitely many octaves requires hitting an invariant set of infinite codimension in $X$; combined with the no-cycle clause the set is empty, and no Leray-Hopf solution blows up.

The two clauses are the imported structure: per-gate error amplification ($\kappa > 1$, no recovery map) plus no error-correcting code (no stable replication cycle). Either clause alone is insufficient; the conjunction is the conjecture.

**Cross-field source.**

1. The threshold theorem (Aharonov-Ben-Or 1997; Knill-Laflamme-Zurek 1998; Kitaev 1997): there is a constant $p_{th} > 0$ such that any quantum circuit of size $N$ can be executed to accuracy $\epsilon$ with per-gate noise $p < p_{th}$, at overhead $\mathrm{polylog}(N/\epsilon)$, USING error-correcting codes and a supply of fresh ancilla qubits.
2. The converse structure, which is what this card actually imports: (a) noisy computation without fresh ancillas has logarithmically bounded depth regardless of error rate (Aharonov-Ben-Or-Impagliazzo-Nisan 1996: entropy accumulates and the state trivializes); (b) above threshold, noise wins at every scale and deep circuits trivialize (Razborov 2003; Kempe-Regev-Unger-de Wolf 2008; Stilck Franca-Garcia-Patron 2021).
3. The map to fluids is nearly literal via Tao's fluid-computing program (Tao, JAMS 2016; Tao, "Searching for singularities in the Navier-Stokes equations," Nat. Rev. Phys. 2019): the averaged equation blows up precisely by building a self-replicating machine that pumps energy down one octave per generation, faster each time.

Imported dictionary: gate layer = octave generation at scale $2^{-j}$; per-gate noise = viscous loss fraction per turnover $= \mathrm{Re}_j^{-1}$; computation depth = number of octaves to scale zero ($\infty$ for blow-up); error correction = a contracting (stable) replication cycle of the octave map; fresh ancillas = an external low-entropy supply (forcing or an un-depleted large-scale reservoir).

**Physics heuristic.** A finite-time singularity is an infinite-depth computation executed in finite time: the flow must hand a working copy of its cascade state down one octave, infinitely often, against viscous noise. Forced stationary turbulence performs a DEEP but not infinite computation and it has fresh ancillas: the forcing re-supplies clean large-scale energy every turnover, which is exactly why constant-flux inertial ranges (K41) can run at constant per-octave efficiency for many decades without contradiction. A blow-up transient has no such supply: by the time generation $n$ fires, only the energy already in flight is available, and any infidelity in the handed-down state cannot be refreshed from upstream because upstream is already spent and slower (the timing is geometric). The flow would have to error-correct using only its own dynamics. Quantum computing says that is possible only with an engineered code; Tao's construction is exactly the engineering of such a code into the nonlinearity. The conjecture says the true nonlinearity is not such a code: pressure nonlocality and triad phase mixing make the octave map error-amplifying, and amplified errors detune the next transfer, compounding until the front falls below the Reynolds threshold and viscosity ends the computation.

**3D mechanism engaged.** Sustained positive flux $\Pi_j > 0$ at constant fidelity through many octaves is the signature of forward energy cascade, whose physical agent is vortex stretching ($\omega \cdot \nabla u$): the trilinear flux integrand is, after integration by parts, the strain-stretching correlation. ## Card 2 (Lens B)

**Name**: Circulation-quantum halo (the uncertainty-principle core floor and the finite halo budget).

**Status (self-assessed)**: alive. The kinematic core is a provable lemma (elementary, stated below, a VERIFIER target); all four gates pass non-vacuously; the named wound (the sheet evasion) is recorded under how-it-dies.

**Formal statement.**

Lemma (halo lower bound; kinematic, claimed provable). There is a universal $c_0 > 0$ such that: let $u \in H^1(B(x_0, R); \mathbb{R}^3)$ be divergence-free, $\omega = \nabla \times u$. Suppose there is a unit vector $e$ and a $C^1$ curve $\gamma \ni x_0$ of length $\ell \le R/4$ with tangent everywhere within angle $\pi/6$ of $e$, and numbers $0 < a < \ell/4$, $\Gamma > 0$, such that for every $y \in \gamma$ and every $s \in [a, \ell/4]$ the flux through the transverse disc satisfies $\left| \int_{D(y, s, e)} \omega \cdot e \; dA \right| \ge \Gamma$. Then
$$\int_{B(x_0, R)} |u|^2 \, dx \;\ge\; \frac{c_0}{2\pi} \, \Gamma^2 \, \ell \, \ln\!\left(\frac{\ell}{4a}\right).$$
Proof mechanism: Stokes converts flux to circulation, Cauchy-Schwarz on each circle gives $\int_{\partial D(y,s,e)} |u|^2 \, dl \ge (\oint u \cdot dl)^2 / (2\pi s) \ge \Gamma^2 / (2\pi s)$, and integrating over $s \in [a, \ell/4]$ (the log) and $y$ along the middle half of $\gamma$ (the factor $\ell$), with the cone condition controlling overlaps, gives the bound. Dyadic restatement: the annular shells $s \in [2^{-m-1}\ell, 2^{-m}\ell]$ are disjoint regions each carrying energy $\ge \frac{c_0 \ln 2}{2\pi} \Gamma^2 \ell$; concentration pays a CONSTANT energy price per octave of core shrinkage, at fixed $(\Gamma, \ell)$. Corollary (core floor): $2 E_0 \ge \frac{c_0}{2\pi} \Gamma^2 \ell \ln(\ell / 4a)$ forces $a \ge \tfrac{\ell}{4} \exp\!\big( -4\pi E_0 / (c_0 \Gamma^2 \ell) \big) > 0$: at fixed circulation and length, finite energy sets a positive core radius, the classical shadow of the quantum-vortex healing length.

Conjecture (the dynamic statement). There exist universal $R_* > 0$ and, for each modulus of continuity $\theta$, a scale $\delta_0(\theta) > 0$, such that no Leray-Hopf solution with energy $E_0$ blows up at $T < \infty$ while satisfying all three of:

- (H1, endpoint coherence) the vorticity direction $\xi = \omega / |\omega|$ admits the FIXED modulus $\theta$ (no Holder rate, $\beta = 0$ in the LEARNINGS #12 ladder) on the intense set $\Omega_t = \{ |\omega(t)| \ge \tfrac12 \|\omega(t)\|_\infty \}$, uniformly for $t$ near $T$;
- (H2, Type-I) $\|\omega(t)\|_\infty \le C_I / (T - t)$;
- (H3, tubality) $\liminf_{t \to T} \Gamma_{\mathrm{act}}(t) / \nu \ge R_*$, where $\Gamma_{\mathrm{act}}(t) = \sup \{ |\int_D \omega \cdot n \, dA| : D \text{ a disc of radius} \le (\nu (T-t))^{1/2} \text{ meeting } \Omega_t \}$.

Mechanism of the contradiction: (H1) forbids closed intense vortex lines of small diameter (a loop of radius $r$ in $\Omega_t$ contains direction swings $|\xi(x) - \xi(x')| = 2$ at separation $2r$, violating $\theta$ once $\theta(2r) < 2$), and since vortex lines cannot end ($\nabla \cdot \omega = 0$), the intense tube through the concentration point extends coherently to length $\ge \delta_0(\theta)$, with (H1) also supplying the cone condition at scales below $\delta_0$. (H3) feeds $\Gamma \ge R_* \nu$ into the Lemma along that tube; blow-up with the known rate $\|\omega\|_\infty \ge c/(T-t)$ forces the core scale $a(t) \to 0$; the Lemma then demands $2 E_0 \ge \frac{c_0}{2\pi} R_*^2 \nu^2 \delta_0 \ln(\delta_0 / 4a(t)) \to \infty$, a contradiction in finitely many octaves. Quantitative form: the number of core-halvings affordable is $N \le 4\pi E_0 / (c_0 \ln 2 \cdot \Gamma^2 \ell)$; with $(\Gamma, \ell) = (R_* \nu, \delta_0)$ this bounds $\|\omega\|_\infty$ and, via BKM, closes regularity UNDER H1-H3. Equivalently: every actual blow-up must be decircularizing ($\Gamma_{\mathrm{act}} / \nu \not\ge R_*$: circulation-dilute at the active scale, e.g. sheets), or incoherent at the $\beta = 0$ endpoint, or Type-II.

**Cross-field source.** (1) Hardy's inequality in $\mathbb{R}^3$: $\int |f|^2 / |x|^2 \, dx \le 4 \int |\nabla f|^2 \, dx$; Heisenberg: $\|f\|_2^2 \le C \| |x| f \|_2 \|\nabla f\|_2$: concentration costs kinetic energy at a rate set by an inverse length. (2) The quantum vortex (Gross-Pitaevskii / Madelung $\psi = \sqrt{\rho} e^{iS/\hbar}$, $v = \nabla S / m$): circulation is quantized, $\Gamma = n h / m$, and a vortex line of winding $n$ has energy per unit length $\pi \rho (\hbar / m)^2 n^2 \ln(R / \xi)$ with core size floored at the healing length $\xi = \hbar / \sqrt{2 m g \rho}$. The card imports exactly this log: in classical NS the quantization of $\Gamma$ is replaced by hypothesis H3 (circulation maintained above the viscous quantum $\nu$, the only circulation scale the equation owns), and the healing length is replaced by the Lemma's core floor $a \ge \frac{\ell}{4} e^{-4\pi E_0 / (c_0 \Gamma^2 \ell)}$.
