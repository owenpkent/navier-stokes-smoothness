# Research directions

Five numbered, attackable directions. Each is a piece of the supercriticality gap with explicit success criteria and the controls it must pass. None is expected to close the problem alone; each is a rung on the ladder, and each is a real contribution if reached.

| # | Direction | Architecture | The bet |
|---|---|---|---|
| [01](01_critical_continuation_criteria.md) | Sharpen the critical continuation criteria | 2 | Push a critical criterion (PSL endpoint, ESS, BKM) toward something the energy can reach, or weaken it logarithmically. |
| [02](02_vorticity_geometry.md) | Vorticity-direction and geometric regularity | 2 | A scale-aware geometric condition (Constantin-Fefferman) is the closest thing to critical control that uses stretching; sharpen it. |
| [03](03_supercriticality_gap.md) | Attack the supercriticality gap directly | 3 | Find a new conserved or coercive quantity at the critical scaling level. The hardest and highest-value direction. |
| [04](04_blowup_and_barriers.md) | Blow-up scenarios and the averaged-NS barrier | 4 | Either construct NS blow-up evading the $L^3$ exclusion, or extract from Tao's barrier exactly what structure a regularity proof must use. |
| [05](05_convex_integration_boundary.md) | The convex-integration boundary | 5 | Map precisely what non-uniqueness does and does not say, and where the Onsager threshold separates it from the regularity question. |

Each spec ends with the three controls (2D smoothness, criticality bookkeeper, viscosity) it must satisfy, so a candidate can be checked before it is believed.
